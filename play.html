Kitchen Table: digital

Play Magic with decklists instead of real cards. Paste your deck, share the table code, and play on a shared digital table with voice chat. You play by the rules as at a real table; the table handles the cards. Playing with real cards on webcams? Use the webcam table.

Voice chat only; no webcams and no voice commands. Everything about the cards is on the table.
